How does Blast know what my child's teacher expects?

Blast reads your child's lectures, notes, textbooks, and syllabus uploads. Using natural language processing, it identifies which sub-topics the teacher spends the most time on and tests frequently, aligning the study plan directly with school expectations.

Is Blast compatible with both CBSE and ICSE boards?

Yes, completely. Blast includes comprehensive syllabus mappings for both CBSE and ICSE across Grades 6 through 12 for Mathematics, Science, English, and Social Studies.

What is the Learning Goldilocks Zone?

The Learning Goldilocks Zone is the optimal difficulty threshold where material is neither too easy (which causes boredom and passive skimming) nor too hard (which causes frustration and cognitive overload). Blast's dynamic calibration adjusts question difficulty in real time to keep your child in the peak retention zone (85% success threshold).

How does billing work after the 7-day free trial?
